September 18, 2000: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

October 18, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Pori (Finland).

February 26, 2007: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings to the Chicago Blackhawks for Kyle Calder.

July 14, 2008: Signed as a Free Agent with the Atlanta Thrashers.

January 14, 2009: Traded by the Atlanta Thrashers to the Columbus Blue Jackets for 6th round draft pick in 2009 (David Pacan) and Clay Wilson.

August 4, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

December 26, 2010: Signed to a PTO (professional tryout) contract by Connecticut (AHL)

February 12, 2011: Signed as a Free Agent with the Dallas Stars.

July 26, 2011: Signed as a Free Agent with the Pittsburgh Penguins.

May 29, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with Ambri-Piotta (Swiss).
