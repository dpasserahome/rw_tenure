October 1, 1998: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings to the New York Rangers for 3rd round draft pick in 2000 (Igor Radulov).

March 10, 2000: Traded by the New York Rangers to the Boston Bruins for Rob DiMaio.

July 3, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with the Philadelphia Flyers.

August 2, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Linkopings (Sweden).

July 1, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Washington Capitals.

January 18, 2013: Signed to a PTO (professional tryout) contract by Grand Rapids (AHL)

January 25, 2013: Signed as a Free Agent with the Philadelphia Flyers.
