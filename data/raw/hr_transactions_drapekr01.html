October 13, 1987: Traded by the Montreal Canadiens (as 3rd round draft pick in 1989) with Gaston Gingras to the St. Louis Blues for 3rd round draft pick in 1989 (Pierre Sévigny) and Larry Trader.

June 17, 1989: Traded by the St. Louis Blues (as 3rd round draft pick in 1989, originally owned by MTL) with Greg Paslawski to the Winnipeg Jets for 3rd round draft pick in 1989 (Denny Felsner) and 2nd round draft pick in 1991 (Steve Staios).

June 30, 1993: Traded by the Winnipeg Jets to the Detroit Red Wings for unknown compensation.

July 26, 2011: Retired.
