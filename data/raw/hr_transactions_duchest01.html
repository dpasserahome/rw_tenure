October 1, 1984: Signed as a Free Agent with the Los Angeles Kings.

May 30, 1991: Traded by the Los Angeles Kings with 4th round draft pick in 1991 (Aris Brimanis) and Steve Kasper to the Philadelphia Flyers for Jeff Chychrun and Jari Kurri.

June 30, 1992: Traded by the Philadelphia Flyers with players to be named later, $15 million, Peter Forsberg, Ron Hextall, Kerry Huffman, Mike Ricci and 1st round draft pick in 1993 (Jocelyn Thibault) to QBN (NHL) for Eric Lindros. The Philadelphia Flyers sent Chris Simon (July 21, 1992) and Nolan Baumgartner (July 21, 1992) to QBN (NHL) to complete the trade.

January 23, 1994: Traded by QBN (NHL) with Denis Chassé to the St. Louis Blues for Bob Bassen, Garth Butcher and Ron Sutter.

August 4, 1995: Traded by the St. Louis Blues to the Ottawa Senators for 2nd round draft pick in 1996 (Cory Sarich).

August 25, 1997: Traded by the Ottawa Senators to the St. Louis Blues for Igor Kravchuk.

July 2, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the Los Angeles Kings.

March 23, 1999: Traded by the Los Angeles Kings to the Philadelphia Flyers for 5th round draft pick in 2000 and Dave Babych.

September 3, 1999: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

October 20, 2002: Retired.
