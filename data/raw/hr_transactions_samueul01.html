March 4, 1991: Traded by the Hartford Whalers with Ron Francis and Grant Jennings to the Pittsburgh Penguins for John Cullen, Jeff Parker and Zarley Zalapski.

August 31, 1995: Traded by the Pittsburgh Penguins with Luc Robitaille to the New York Rangers for Petr Nedvěd and Sergei Zubov.

March 23, 1999: Traded by the New York Rangers to the Detroit Red Wings for 2nd round draft pick in 1999 and 3rd round draft pick in 2000 (Igor Radulov).

June 25, 1999: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings to the Atlanta Thrashers for unknown compensation.

October 19, 1999: Signed as a Free Agent with the Philadelphia Flyers.

February 12, 2001: Retired.
