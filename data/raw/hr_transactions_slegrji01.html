April 7, 1995: Traded by the Vancouver Canucks to the Edmonton Oilers for Roman Oksiuta.

August 12, 1997: Traded by the Edmonton Oilers to the Pittsburgh Penguins for 3rd round draft pick in 1998 (Brian Gionta).

January 14, 2001: Traded by the Pittsburgh Penguins to the Atlanta Thrashers for 3rd round draft pick in 2001 (Aaron Johnson).

March 19, 2002: Traded by the Atlanta Thrashers to the Detroit Red Wings for Yuri Butsayev and 3rd round draft pick in 2002 (Jeff Genovy).

September 4, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Vancouver Canucks.

January 17, 2004: Traded by the Vancouver Canucks to the Boston Bruins for unknown compensation.

August 20, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Litvinov (Czechia).

July 31, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with Litvinov (Czechia).
