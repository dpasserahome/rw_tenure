February 15, 1995: Signed as a Free Agent with the Florida Panthers.

November 19, 1996: Traded by the Florida Panthers with Stu Barnes to the Pittsburgh Penguins for Chris Wells.

September 24, 1997: Traded by the Pittsburgh Penguins to the Buffalo Sabres for 5th round draft pick in 1998 (Rob Scuderi).

November 16, 2002: Traded by the Buffalo Sabres to the Detroit Red Wings for unknown compensation.

February 18, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with Flint (UHL).
