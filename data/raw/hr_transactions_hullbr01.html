March 7, 1988: Traded by the Calgary Flames with Steve Bozek to the St. Louis Blues for Rob Ramage and Rick Wamsley.

July 3, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the Dallas Stars.

August 22, 2001: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

August 6, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with the Phoenix Coyotes.

October 15, 2005: Retired.
