August 15, 1996: Traded by the Dallas Stars (as 5th round draft pick in 1998) to the San Jose Sharks for Sergei Gorbachev.

June 24, 2001: Traded by the San Jose Sharks with Christian Gosselin to the New York Rangers for future considerations and Adam Graves.

February 10, 2003: Traded by the New York Rangers with Joël Bouchard, Rico Fata and Richard Lintner to the Pittsburgh Penguins for Alex Kovalev, Dan LaCouture, Janne Laukkanen and Mike Wilson.

June 21, 2003: Traded by the Pittsburgh Penguins with 1st round draft pick in 2003 (Nathan Horton) and 2nd round draft pick in 2003 (Stefan Meyer) to the Florida Panthers for 3rd round draft pick in 2003 (Daniel Carcillo) and 1st round draft pick in 2003 (Marc-André Fleury).

September 8, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Geneve (Swiss).

October 26, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Sodertalje (Sweden).

September 17, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 3, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Vancouver Canucks.

October 22, 2011: Traded by the Vancouver Canucks with Marco Sturm to the Florida Panthers for David Booth, 3rd round draft pick in 2013 (Cole Cassels) and Steve Reinprecht.

July 1, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

March 26, 2015: Retired.
