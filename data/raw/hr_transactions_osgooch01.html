September 28, 2001: Claimed by the New York Islanders from the Detroit Red Wings in Waiver Draft.

March 11, 2003: Traded by the New York Islanders with 3rd round draft pick in 2003 (Konstantin Barulin) to the St. Louis Blues for 2nd round draft pick in 2003 (Jeremy Colliton) and Justin Papineau.

August 8, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 19, 2011: Retired.
