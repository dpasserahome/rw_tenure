October 25, 1991: Traded by the Buffalo Sabres with Benoît Hogue, Dave McLlwain and Pierre Turgeon to the New York Islanders for Randy Hillier, Pat LaFontaine, 4th round draft pick in 1992 (Dean Melanson) and Randy Wood.

June 28, 1994: Traded by the New York Islanders with 1st round draft pick in 1994 (Wade Belak) to QBN (NHL) for 1st round draft pick in 1994 (Brett Lindros) and Ron Sutter.

June 26, 1998: Drafted by the Nashville Predators from the Colorado Avalanche in the 1998 expansion draft.

July 7, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 19, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with the Atlanta Thrashers.

April 27, 2003: Retired.
