June 14, 1996: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs to the San Jose Sharks for 5th round draft pick in 1996 and Jamie Baker.

March 24, 1998: Traded by the San Jose Sharks to the St. Louis Blues for Joe Murphy.

December 30, 1998: Selected off waivers by the Detroit Red Wings from the St. Louis Blues.

July 21, 1999: Signed as a Free Agent with the Phoenix Coyotes.

March 13, 2000: Traded by the Phoenix Coyotes to the Detroit Red Wings for Philippe Audet.

July 24, 2001: Signed as a Free Agent with the Colorado Avalanche.

February 12, 2002: Released by the Colorado Avalanche.

March 5, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Chicago Blackhawks.

August 20, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Florida Panthers.

December 26, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with Weiswasser (German-3).
