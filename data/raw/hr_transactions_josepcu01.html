June 16, 1989: Signed as a Free Agent with the St. Louis Blues.

August 4, 1995: Traded by the St. Louis Blues with Mike Grier to the Edmonton Oilers for 1st round draft pick in 1997 and 1st round draft pick in 1996 (Marty Reasoner).

July 15, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the Toronto Maple Leafs.

June 30, 2002: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs to the Calgary Flames for future considerations and 3rd round draft pick in 2003 (Danny Irmen).

July 2, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

August 17, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with the Phoenix Coyotes.

January 17, 2008: Signed as a Free Agent with the Calgary Flames.

July 1, 2008: Signed as a Free Agent with the Toronto Maple Leafs.

January 12, 2010: Retired.
