March 8, 1988: Traded by the Boston Bruins with 2nd round draft pick in 1988 and Geoff Courtnall to the Edmonton Oilers for Andy Moog.

January 11, 1996: Traded by the Edmonton Oilers to the Boston Bruins for Sean Brown, Mariusz Czerkawski and 1st round draft pick in 1996 (Matthieu Descoteaux).

March 1, 1997: Traded by the Boston Bruins with Adam Oates and Rick Tocchet to the Washington Capitals for 2nd round draft pick in 1998 (Bobby Allen), Jason Allison, Jim Carey, Anson Carter and 3rd round draft pick in 1997 (Lee Goren).

June 18, 1998: Traded by the Washington Capitals to the Tampa Bay Lightning for 3rd round draft pick in 1998 and 2nd round draft pick in 1999 (Michal Sivek).

March 23, 1999: Traded by the Tampa Bay Lightning to the Detroit Red Wings for unknown compensation.

August 4, 1999: Signed as a Free Agent with the Edmonton Oilers.

April 24, 2000: Retired.
