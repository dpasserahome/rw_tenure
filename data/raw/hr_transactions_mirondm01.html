June 16, 1990: Traded by the Philadelphia Flyers (as 8th round draft pick in 1991) with Kevin Maguire to the Toronto Maple Leafs for 3rd round draft pick in 1990 .

July 8, 1995: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs with 2nd round draft pick in 1996 to the Pittsburgh Penguins for Larry Murphy.

November 19, 1996: Traded by the Pittsburgh Penguins with Shawn Antoski to ANA (NHL) for Alex Hicks and Fredrik Olausson.

March 24, 1998: Traded by ANA (NHL) to the Detroit Red Wings for 4th round draft pick in 1998 and Jamie Pushor.

July 29, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the Washington Capitals.
