June 29, 1990: Traded by the Montreal Canadiens with 2nd round draft pick in 1991 to the Chicago Blackhawks for Denis Savard.

March 23, 1999: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks to the Detroit Red Wings for Anders Eriksson, 1st round draft pick in 1999 (Steve McCarthy) and 1st round draft pick in 2001 (Adam Munro).

February 1, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with Motor City (UHL).

October 20, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with Chicago (AHL).

March 2, 2010: Signed as a Free Agent with the Atlanta Thrashers.

August 31, 2010: Retired.
