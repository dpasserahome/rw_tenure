October 13, 2008: Selected off waivers by the Los Angeles Kings from the Detroit Red Wings.

July 3, 2009: Traded by the Los Angeles Kings with Tom Preissing and 5th round draft pick in 2010 (Luke Walker) to the Colorado Avalanche for Ryan Smyth.

February 21, 2012: Traded by the Tampa Bay Lightning to the Detroit Red Wings for Sebastien Piche and 1st round draft pick in 2012 (Andrei Vasilevskiy).

February 21, 2012: Traded by the Colorado Avalanche to the Tampa Bay Lightning for Steve Downie.

April 2, 2012: Suspended Charging Tomas Kopecky

October 12, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with Denver (CHL).

September 28, 2016: Signed as a Free Agent with the New Jersey Devils.

March 1, 2017: Traded by the New Jersey Devils to the Columbus Blue Jackets for Dalton Prout.

July 1, 2017: Signed as a Free Agent with the Minnesota Wild.

August 28, 2018: Signed as a Free Agent with HIFK Helsinki (Finland).
