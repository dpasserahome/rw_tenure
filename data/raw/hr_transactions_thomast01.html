May 12, 1984: Signed as a Free Agent with the Toronto Maple Leafs.

September 3, 1987: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs with Bob McGill and Rick Vaive to the Chicago Blackhawks for Eddie Olczyk and Al Secord.

October 25, 1991: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks with Adam Creighton to the New York Islanders for Brad Lauer and Brent Sutter.

October 3, 1995: Traded by the New York Islanders to the New Jersey Devils for Claude Lemieux.

July 30, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the Toronto Maple Leafs.

July 17, 2001: Signed as a Free Agent with the Chicago Blackhawks.

March 11, 2003: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks to ANA (NHL) for 5th round draft pick in 2003 (Alexei Ivanov).

November 5, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.
