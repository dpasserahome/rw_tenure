June 27, 1998: Traded by the Nashville Predators (as 1st round draft pick in 1998) with 2nd round draft pick in 1998 (Jonathan Cheechoo) to the San Jose Sharks for 3rd round draft pick in 1998 and 1st round draft pick in 1998 (David Legwand).

January 15, 1999: Traded by Regina (WHL) to Calgary (WHL) for Donald Choukalos and Ryan Geremia.

November 30, 2005: Traded by the San Jose Sharks with Wayne Primeau and Marco Sturm to the Boston Bruins for Joe Thornton.

February 10, 2007: Traded by the Boston Bruins with 4th round draft pick in 2008 (T.J. Brodie) and Wayne Primeau to the Calgary Flames for Andrew Ference and Chuck Kobasew.

July 3, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with the Los Angeles Kings.

February 26, 2008: Traded by the Los Angeles Kings to the Detroit Red Wings for 4th round draft pick in 2009 (Ben Chiarot) and 2nd round draft pick in 2008 (Peter Delmas).

June 10, 2012: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings to the San Jose Sharks for 7th round draft pick in 2014 (Alexander Kadeykin) and Andrew Murray.

October 9, 2013: Suspended Illegal check to the head of Rick Nash

July 1, 2014: Traded by the San Jose Sharks to the Colorado Avalanche for 2nd round draft pick in 2016 (Cameron Morrison) and 6th round draft pick in 2017 (Denis Smirnov).
