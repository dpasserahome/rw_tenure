March 8, 1982: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs (as 5th round draft pick in 1983, originally owned by DET) with 4th round draft pick in 1982 (Craig Coxe) to the Detroit Red Wings for Jim Korn.

March 5, 1991: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings with Pär Djoos to the New York Rangers for Jim Cummins, Kevin Miller and Dennis Vial.

March 20, 1996: Traded by the New York Rangers to the Vancouver Canucks for Kay Whitmore.

December 27, 1996: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

October 10, 2000: Retired.
