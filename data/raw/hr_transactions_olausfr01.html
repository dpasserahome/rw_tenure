December 6, 1993: Traded by the Winnipeg Jets with 7th round draft pick in 1994 to the Edmonton Oilers for 3rd round draft pick in 1994 (Tavis Hansen).

January 16, 1996: Selected off waivers by ANA (NHL) from the Edmonton Oilers.

November 19, 1996: Traded by ANA (NHL) with Alex Hicks to the Pittsburgh Penguins for Shawn Antoski and Dmitri Mironov.

August 28, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with ANA (NHL).

May 24, 2001: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 12, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with ANA (NHL).

July 9, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with HV 71 (Sweden).
