March 6, 2002: Traded by the Boston Bruins (as 3rd round draft pick in 2002) to ANA (NHL) for Marty McInnis.

June 22, 2002: Traded by the Nashville Predators (as 3rd round draft pick in 2002, originally owned by BOS) to the Detroit Red Wings for 3rd round draft pick in 2003 (Grigory Shafigulin).

June 22, 2002: Traded by ANA (NHL) (as 3rd round draft pick in 2002, originally owned by BOS Nashville agreed not to select Joffrey Lupul in the 2002 NHL Draft) to the Nashville Predators for unknown compensation.

September 21, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with Jokerit Helsinki (Finland).

July 5, 2013: Signed as a Free Agent with the Tampa Bay Lightning.

March 1, 2017: Traded by the Tampa Bay Lightning with 7th round draft pick in 2017 (Conditional) (Wyatt Kalynuk) and 4th round draft pick in 2017 (Maksim Sushko) to the Philadelphia Flyers for Mark Streit.

July 1, 2018: Signed as a Free Agent with the New York Islanders.

July 1, 2019: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

April 29, 2025: Retired.
