October 1, 1997: Signed as a Free Agent with the Edmonton Oilers.

November 6, 1997: Selected off waivers by the Florida Panthers from the Edmonton Oilers.

March 13, 2001: Traded by the Florida Panthers with future considerations to the Columbus Blue Jackets for Kevyn Adams and 4th round draft pick in 2001 (Mike Woodford).

July 30, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

August 7, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with the Carolina Hurricanes.

July 1, 2010: Signed as a Free Agent with the Phoenix Coyotes.

July 1, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with the Dallas Stars.

January 21, 2015: Retired.
