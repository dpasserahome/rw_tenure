August 17, 1992: Signed as a Free Agent with the Chicago Blackhawks.

June 16, 1993: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

March 23, 1999: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings with 2nd round draft pick in 1999 (Sheldon Keefe) to the Tampa Bay Lightning for Wendel Clark and 6th round draft pick in 1999 (Kent McDonell).

June 2, 2000: Traded by the Tampa Bay Lightning to the Montreal Canadiens for 7th round draft pick in 2000 .

May 29, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with the Tampa Bay Lightning.

January 16, 2003: Retired.

November 6, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with Jokerit Helsinki (Finland).
