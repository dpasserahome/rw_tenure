July 9, 2001: Traded by the Carolina Hurricanes (as 2nd round draft pick in 2002) to the Detroit Red Wings for Aaron Ward.

December 2, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Vsetin (Czechia).

July 10, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with Dynamo Moscow (KHL).

May 24, 2010: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 2, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with the Calgary Flames.

September 20, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with Lev Praha (KHL).

December 19, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with Trinec (Czechia).

February 27, 2016: Traded by the Calgary Flames to the Florida Panthers for 4th round draft pick in 2018 (Demetrios Koumontzis) and 2nd round draft pick in 2016 (Tyler Parsons).

August 24, 2016: Signed as a Free Agent with the Dallas Stars.
