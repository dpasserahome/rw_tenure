January 30, 1983: Signed as a Free Agent with the Calgary Flames.

January 2, 1992: Traded by the Calgary Flames with Doug Gilmour, Kent Manderville, Ric Nattress and Rick Wamsley to the Toronto Maple Leafs for Craig Berube, Alexander Godynyuk, Gary Leeman, Michel Petit and Jeff Reese.

March 24, 1998: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs to the Detroit Red Wings for 4th round draft pick in 1998 (Alexei Ponikarovsky).
