June 28, 1994: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs with 1st round draft pick in 1994 , Sylvain Lefebvre and Landon Wilson to QBN (NHL) for 1st round draft pick in 1994 (Nolan Baumgartner), Garth Butcher, Mats Sundin and Todd Warriner.

October 3, 1995: Traded by the Colorado Avalanche to the New York Islanders for Claude Lemieux.

March 13, 1996: Traded by the New York Islanders with Mathieu Schneider and D.J. Smith to the Toronto Maple Leafs for Sean Haggerty, Darby Hendrickson, Kenny Jönsson and 1st round draft pick in 1997 (Roberto Luongo).

July 31, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the Tampa Bay Lightning.

March 23, 1999: Traded by the Tampa Bay Lightning with 6th round draft pick in 1999 (Kent McDonell) to the Detroit Red Wings for Kevin Hodson and 2nd round draft pick in 1999 (Sheldon Keefe).

August 2, 1999: Signed as a Free Agent with the Chicago Blackhawks.

January 14, 2000: Signed as a Free Agent with the Toronto Maple Leafs.

January 14, 2000: Released by the Chicago Blackhawks.

January 14, 2000: Released by the Chicago Blackhawks.

June 29, 2000: Retired.
