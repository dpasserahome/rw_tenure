March 24, 1998: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings with 4th round draft pick in 1998 to ANA (NHL) for Dmitri Mironov.

June 25, 1999: Drafted by the Atlanta Thrashers from ANA (NHL) in the 1999 expansion draft.

July 15, 1999: Traded by the Atlanta Thrashers to the Dallas Stars for Jason Botterill.

June 23, 2000: Drafted by the Columbus Blue Jackets from the Dallas Stars in the 2000 expansion draft.

March 15, 2002: Traded by the Columbus Blue Jackets to the Pittsburgh Penguins for 4th round draft pick in 2003 (Kevin Jarman).

November 18, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with Syracuse (AHL).

December 10, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Columbus Blue Jackets.

January 23, 2004: Traded by the Columbus Blue Jackets to the New York Rangers for 8th round draft pick in 2004 (Matt Greer).

August 9, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Syracuse (AHL).

March 9, 2006: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings to the Phoenix Coyotes for unknown compensation.

July 21, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with the Columbus Blue Jackets.
