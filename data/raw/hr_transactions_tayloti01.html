January 29, 1993: Traded by the Washington Capitals to the Vancouver Canucks for Eric Murano.

July 28, 1993: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

September 28, 1997: Claimed by the Boston Bruins from the Detroit Red Wings in Waiver Draft.

July 30, 1999: Signed as a Free Agent with the New York Rangers.

June 30, 2001: Traded by the New York Rangers to the Tampa Bay Lightning for Nils Ekman and Kyle Freadrich.
