June 17, 1989: Traded by the New Jersey Devils to the Hartford Whalers for Sylvain Turgeon.

March 23, 1995: Traded by the Hartford Whalers to the New York Rangers for Michael Stewart, 4th round draft pick in 1996 , Glen Featherstone and 1st round draft pick in 1995 (Jean-Sébastien Giguère).

August 21, 1996: Signed as a Free Agent with the Dallas Stars.

November 11, 1999: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

August 31, 2001: Signed as a Free Agent with the Dallas Stars.
