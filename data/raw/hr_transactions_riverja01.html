September 27, 1999: Claimed by the New York Islanders from the St. Louis Blues in Waiver Draft.

November 30, 2000: Signed as a Free Agent with the Ottawa Senators.

October 13, 2001: Selected off waivers by the Boston Bruins from the Ottawa Senators.

November 2, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with San Antonio (AHL).

December 16, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with the Florida Panthers.

July 29, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

November 3, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Hershey (AHL).

March 9, 2006: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings to the Phoenix Coyotes for 7th round draft pick in 2006 (Nick Oslund).

August 18, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with the St. Louis Blues.

July 5, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with the Montreal Canadiens.

October 12, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with Spartak Moscow (Russia).

December 16, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with Ambri-Piotta (Swiss).
