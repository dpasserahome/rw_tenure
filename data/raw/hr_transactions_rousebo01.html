March 7, 1989: Traded by the Minnesota North Stars with Dino Ciccarelli to the Washington Capitals for Mike Gartner and Larry Murphy.

January 16, 1991: Traded by the Washington Capitals with Peter Zezel to the Toronto Maple Leafs for Al Iafrate.

August 5, 1994: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 13, 1998: Signed as a Free Agent with the San Jose Sharks.

December 26, 1999: Released by the San Jose Sharks.
