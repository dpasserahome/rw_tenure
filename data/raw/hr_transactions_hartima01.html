March 27, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with the Atlanta Thrashers.

July 15, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Columbus Blue Jackets.

January 26, 2007: Traded by the Columbus Blue Jackets with Joe Motzko and 4th round draft pick in 2007 (Sebastian Stefaniszin) to the Anaheim Ducks for Curtis Glencross, Zenon Konopka and 7th round draft pick in 2007 (Trent Vogelhuber).

July 16, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 22, 2008: Signed as a Free Agent with Riga (KHL).

July 30, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with CSKA Moscow (KHL).
