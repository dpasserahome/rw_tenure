June 11, 1993: Traded by the Winnipeg Jets with a player to be named later, future considerations (Alan Kerr) and 4th round draft pick in 1993 (John Jakopin) to the Detroit Red Wings for Paul Ysebaert. The Winnipeg Jets sent Alan Kerr (June 15, 1993) to the Detroit Red Wings to complete the trade.

July 9, 2001: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings to the Carolina Hurricanes for 2nd round draft pick in 2002 (Jiří Hudler).

February 15, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with Ingolstadt (Germany).

July 3, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with the New York Rangers.

February 27, 2007: Traded by the New York Rangers to the Boston Bruins for Paul Mara.

July 24, 2009: Traded by the Boston Bruins to the Carolina Hurricanes for 4th round draft pick in 2010 (Craig Cunningham) and Patrick Eaves.

March 3, 2010: Traded by the Carolina Hurricanes to the Anaheim Ducks for Justin Pogge and 4th round draft pick in 2010 (Justin Shugg).

August 24, 2010: Retired.
