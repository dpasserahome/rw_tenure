June 19, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Dynamo Moscow (Russia).

September 22, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with CSKA Moscow (KHL).

June 24, 2016: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings with 1st round draft pick in 2016 (Jakob Chychrun) to the Arizona Coyotes for 1st round draft pick in 2016 (Dennis Cholowski), 2nd round draft pick in 2016 (Filip Hronek) and Joe Vitale.

June 24, 2016: Retired.

July 8, 2016: Signed as a Free Agent with St. Petersburg (KHL).

June 5, 2019: Signed as a Free Agent with Avtomobilist Yekaterinburg (KHL).

July 2, 2022: Retired.
