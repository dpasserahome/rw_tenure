August 27, 1992: Traded by the Montreal Canadiens with Shayne Corson and Vladimír Vůjtek to the Edmonton Oilers for 4th round draft pick in 1993 and Vincent Damphousse.

March 5, 1993: Traded by the Edmonton Oilers to the Minnesota North Stars for Todd Elik.

August 1, 1997: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

October 5, 1998: Traded by the Tampa Bay Lightning to the Detroit Red Wings for 6th round draft pick in 1999 (Kent McDonell).

October 5, 1998: Claimed by the Tampa Bay Lightning from the Detroit Red Wings in Waiver Draft.

February 13, 2002: Selected off waivers by the Dallas Stars from the Detroit Red Wings.

July 11, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with the Nashville Predators.
