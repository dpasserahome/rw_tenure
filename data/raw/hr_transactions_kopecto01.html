June 24, 2000: Traded by the New York Rangers (as 2nd round draft pick in 2000) to the Detroit Red Wings for 3rd round draft pick in 2000 (Dominic Moore) and 2nd round draft pick in 2000 (Filip Novák).

July 1, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Chicago Blackhawks.

June 27, 2011: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks to the Florida Panthers for 7th round draft pick in 2012 (Judd Peterson).

October 13, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with Trencin (Slovakia).
