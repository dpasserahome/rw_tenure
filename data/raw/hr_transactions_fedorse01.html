July 19, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with ANA (NHL).

November 15, 2005: Traded by the Anaheim Ducks with 5th round draft pick in 2006 (Maxime Frechette) to the Columbus Blue Jackets for François Beauchemin and Tyler Wright.

February 26, 2008: Traded by the Columbus Blue Jackets to the Washington Capitals for Ted Ruth.

June 25, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with Magnitogorsk (KHL).
