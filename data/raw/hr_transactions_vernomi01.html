June 2, 1980: Traded by the Boston Bruins (as 3rd round draft pick in 1981) with 2nd round draft pick in 1980 (Steve Konroyd) to the Atlanta Flames for Jim Craig.

June 29, 1994: Traded by the Calgary Flames to the Detroit Red Wings for Steve Chiasson.

August 18, 1997: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings with 5th round draft pick in 1999 to the San Jose Sharks for 2nd round draft pick in 1998 and 2nd round draft pick in 1999 (Sheldon Keefe).

December 30, 1999: Traded by the San Jose Sharks with 3rd round draft pick in 2000 to the Florida Panthers for Radek Dvořák.

June 23, 2000: Traded by the Minnesota Wild to the Calgary Flames for Dan Cavanaugh and 8th round draft pick in 2001 (Jake Riddle).

June 23, 2000: Drafted by the Minnesota Wild from the Florida Panthers in the 2000 expansion draft.

September 13, 2002: Retired.
