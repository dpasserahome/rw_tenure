January 20, 1990: Traded by the New York Rangers with Tony Granato to the Los Angeles Kings for Bernie Nicholls.

February 16, 1994: Traded by the Los Angeles Kings with Shawn McEachern to the Pittsburgh Penguins for Marty McSorley and Jim Paek.

January 27, 1997: Traded by the Pittsburgh Penguins to the Detroit Red Wings for Greg Johnson.

October 20, 1997: Signed as a Free Agent with ANA (NHL).
