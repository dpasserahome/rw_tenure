March 20, 1999: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks with Chad Kilger, Christian Laflamme, Ethan Moreau and 2nd round draft pick in 1999 (Alexei Semenov) to the Edmonton Oilers for 2nd round draft pick in 1999 , Jonas Elofsson, Dean McAmmond and Boris Mironov.

July 15, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Phoenix Coyotes.

September 6, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Mora (Sweden).

October 4, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

June 14, 2017: Retired.
