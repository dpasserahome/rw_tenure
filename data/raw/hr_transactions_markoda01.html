June 12, 2001: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs to the Phoenix Coyotes for Travis Green, Craig Mills and Robert Reichel.

June 21, 2003: Traded by the Phoenix Coyotes with 3rd round draft pick in 2004 (Billy Ryan) to the Carolina Hurricanes for Igor Knyazev and David Tanabe.

January 20, 2004: Traded by the Carolina Hurricanes to the Philadelphia Flyers for Justin Williams.

November 15, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Chekhov (Russia-2).

August 2, 2005: Traded by the Philadelphia Flyers to the Nashville Predators for 3rd round draft pick in 2006 (Bud Holloway).

July 26, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

October 26, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with Dynamo Moscow (Russia).
