April 3, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 7, 2010: Signed as a Free Agent with the Toronto Maple Leafs.

July 3, 2011: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs with Robert Slaney and 4th round draft pick in 2013 (Zach Pochiro) to the Nashville Predators for Cody Franson and Matthew Lombardi.

November 7, 2011: Signed as a Free Agent with Springfield (AHL).

January 19, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with the Columbus Blue Jackets.

September 21, 2012: Signed as a Free Agent with Rockford (AHL).

February 5, 2013: Signed as a Free Agent with Binghamton (AHL).
