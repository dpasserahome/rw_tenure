August 7, 1992: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks to the Buffalo Sabres for Stéphane Beauregard and 4th round draft pick in 1993 (Éric Dazé).

July 1, 2001: Traded by the Buffalo Sabres to the Detroit Red Wings for Vyacheslav Kozlov and 1st round draft pick in 2002 (Jim Slater).

June 25, 2002: Retired.

July 6, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with the Ottawa Senators.

July 31, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

June 9, 2008: Retired.

April 21, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with Pardubice (Czechia).

October 9, 2012: Retired.
