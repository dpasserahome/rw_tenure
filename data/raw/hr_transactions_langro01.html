September 2, 1997: Signed as a Free Agent with the Pittsburgh Penguins.

September 28, 1997: Claimed by the Boston Bruins from the Pittsburgh Penguins in Waiver Draft.

October 25, 1997: Selected off waivers by the Pittsburgh Penguins from the Boston Bruins.

July 1, 2002: Signed as a Free Agent with the Washington Capitals.

February 27, 2004: Traded by the Washington Capitals to the Detroit Red Wings for Tomáš Fleischmann, 1st round draft pick in 2004 (Mike Green) and 4th round draft pick in 2006 (Luke Lynes).

July 2, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with the Chicago Blackhawks.

September 12, 2008: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks to the Montreal Canadiens for 2nd round draft pick in 2010 (Jared Knight).

September 29, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Phoenix Coyotes.
