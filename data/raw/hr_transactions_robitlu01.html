July 29, 1994: Traded by the Los Angeles Kings to the Pittsburgh Penguins for 2nd round draft pick in 1995 (Pavel Rosa) and Rick Tocchet.

August 31, 1995: Traded by the Pittsburgh Penguins with Ulf Samuelsson to the New York Rangers for Petr Nedvěd and Sergei Zubov.

August 28, 1997: Traded by the New York Rangers to the Los Angeles Kings for Kevin Stevens.

July 5, 2001: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.

July 24, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Los Angeles Kings.

April 11, 2006: Retired. Effective at end of season
