July 25, 1991: Signed as a Free Agent with the St. Louis Blues.

July 27, 1995: Traded by the St. Louis Blues to the Hartford Whalers for Chris Pronger.

October 9, 1996: Traded by the Hartford Whalers with Brian Glynn to the Detroit Red Wings for Paul Coffey, Keith Primeau and 1st round draft pick in 1997 (Nikos Tselios).

July 9, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with the New York Rangers.

January 15, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the New Jersey Devils.

November 17, 2009: Retired.
