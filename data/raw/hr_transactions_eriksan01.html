March 23, 1999: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings with 1st round draft pick in 1999 (Steve McCarthy) and 1st round draft pick in 2001 (Adam Munro) to the Chicago Blackhawks for Chris Chelios.

November 6, 2000: Traded by the Chicago Blackhawks to the Florida Panthers for Jaroslav Špaček.

July 4, 2001: Signed as a Free Agent with the Toronto Maple Leafs.

October 10, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the Columbus Blue Jackets.

September 16, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with the Calgary Flames.

October 29, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Jonkoping (Sweden).

July 1, 2006: Signed as a Free Agent with the Columbus Blue Jackets.

July 5, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with the Calgary Flames.

December 4, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with San Antonio (AHL).

December 21, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Phoenix Coyotes.

March 3, 2010: Traded by the Phoenix Coyotes to the New York Rangers for Miika Wiikman and 7th round draft pick in 2011 (Zac Larraza).

November 25, 2010: Signed as a Free Agent with Timra (Sweden).

December 19, 2010: Signed as a Free Agent with MODO (Sweden).
