June 20, 1993: Traded by QBN (NHL) (as 1st round draft pick in 1993) with Ron Hextall to the New York Islanders for 1st round draft pick in 1993 (Adam Deadmarsh) and Mark Fitzpatrick.

February 6, 1998: Traded by the New York Islanders with Bryan McCabe and 3rd round draft pick in 1998 (Jarkko Ruutu) to the Vancouver Canucks for Trevor Linden.

March 8, 2004: Suspended After deliberate injury to Steve Moore in game vs. Colorado

June 23, 2006: Traded by the Vancouver Canucks with Bryan Allen and Alex Auld to the Florida Panthers for Lukáš Krajíček, Roberto Luongo and 6th round draft pick in 2006 (Sergei Shirokov).

February 27, 2007: Traded by the Florida Panthers to the Detroit Red Wings for Shawn Matthias and 2nd round draft pick in 2007 (Nick Spaling).

July 2, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with the Anaheim Ducks.

July 7, 2008: Signed as a Free Agent with the Calgary Flames.

August 18, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Detroit Red Wings.
