June 13, 1987: Traded by the New Jersey Devils (as 3rd round draft pick in 1987) to the Montreal Canadiens for David Maley.

April 5, 1995: Traded by the Montreal Canadiens with Craig Darby and Kirk Muller to the New York Islanders for Vladimir Malakhov and Pierre Turgeon.

March 13, 1996: Traded by the New York Islanders with Wendel Clark and D.J. Smith to the Toronto Maple Leafs for Sean Haggerty, Darby Hendrickson, Kenny Jönsson and 1st round draft pick in 1997 (Roberto Luongo).

October 14, 1998: Traded by the Toronto Maple Leafs to the New York Rangers for 4th round draft pick in 1999 and Alexander Karpovtsev.

June 23, 2000: Drafted by the Columbus Blue Jackets from the New York Rangers in the 2000 expansion draft.

August 14, 2000: Signed as a Free Agent with the Los Angeles Kings.

March 11, 2003: Traded by the Los Angeles Kings to the Detroit Red Wings for Sean Avery, 2nd round draft pick in 2004 (Martins Karsums), Maxim Kuznetsov and 1st round draft pick in 2003 (Jeff Tambellini).

July 1, 2007: Signed as a Free Agent with the Anaheim Ducks.

September 26, 2008: Traded by the Anaheim Ducks to the Atlanta Thrashers for Ken Klee, Brad Larsen and Chad Painchaud.

February 16, 2009: Traded by the Atlanta Thrashers with 3rd round draft pick in 2009 (Joonas Nättinen) to the Montreal Canadiens for 3rd round draft pick in 2010 (Julian Melchiori) and 2nd round draft pick in 2009 (Jeremy Morin).

August 28, 2009: Signed as a Free Agent with the Vancouver Canucks.

March 3, 2010: Traded by the Vancouver Canucks to the Phoenix Coyotes for 6th round draft pick in 2010 (Alex Friesen) and Sean Zimmerman.
