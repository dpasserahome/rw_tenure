July 1, 2001: Traded by the Detroit Red Wings with 1st round draft pick in 2002 (Jim Slater) to the Buffalo Sabres for Dominik Hašek.

June 22, 2002: Traded by the Buffalo Sabres with 2nd round draft pick in 2002 (Joakim Lindström) to the Atlanta Thrashers for 3rd round draft pick in 2002 (John Adams) and 2nd round draft pick in 2002 (Jeff Deslauriers).

September 15, 2004: Signed as a Free Agent with Voskresensk (Russia).

February 17, 2005: Signed as a Free Agent with Kazan (Russia).

September 29, 2010: Signed as a Free Agent with CSKA Moscow (KHL).
