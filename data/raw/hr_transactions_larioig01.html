October 4, 1992: Claimed by the San Jose Sharks from the Vancouver Canucks in Waiver Draft.

October 24, 1995: Traded by the San Jose Sharks to the Detroit Red Wings for Ray Sheppard.

July 1, 2000: Signed as a Free Agent with the Florida Panthers.

December 28, 2000: Traded by the Florida Panthers to the Detroit Red Wings for Yan Golubovsky.

September 10, 2003: Signed as a Free Agent with the New Jersey Devils.

April 20, 2004: Retired.
